Audie

Privacy and terms

Last updated November 16, 2026

Audie is a meeting recorder for Mac, with a Windows test build, made by David Jung ("I", "me"). This page says what Audie does with your data and the terms for using it.

The short version

  • Your audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. Audie has no account, no server and no cloud storage. I never receive your recordings or transcripts.
  • Text goes to Anthropic only when Claude reads it. If you ask Claude about a meeting, that transcript text becomes part of your Claude conversation, under your agreement with Anthropic. Audie itself sends nothing to Anthropic.
  • I collect nothing about how you use Audie. No analytics and no tracking. Crash reports stay on your Mac unless you choose to send one.

What Audie stores on your Mac

In ~/Library/Application Support/Audie/, or a meetings folder you choose in Settings › Recording:

WhatKept
Meeting audio30 days by default (7, 90 days or forever in Settings). Never deleted before its transcript is made.
Transcripts, meeting details, how you filed a meeting (section, pinned, archived)Until you delete the meeting
English translations of lines in other languages, if live translation is onUntil you delete the meeting
Settings, your custom words, an activity log (meeting titles and technical events, no transcript text)Until you delete Audie's folder
The speech model (1.5 GB)Until you delete Audie's folder
Crash reports, if Audie has crashedUntil you delete them or Audie's folder

If you pick a meetings folder inside iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that service copies your recordings to its cloud under its own terms. Audie warns you when you pick one.

When Audie uses the internet

Only for these, and none of them include your recordings or transcripts:

  • Updates. About once a day Audie downloads a small update list from GitHub. When there is a new version, Audie downloads and installs it, unless you turn that off in Settings › About. GitHub sees your IP address and Audie's version number, as with any download.
  • The speech model, downloaded once from GitHub, or from Hugging Face if that fails. Whichever one serves the file sees your IP address. You can also import the model from a file.
  • The speaker model (about 20 MB), which tells voices apart after a meeting, downloaded once the same way: from GitHub, or from Hugging Face if that fails. Telling voices apart happens on your Mac; no audio leaves it.
  • Live translation. Done on your Mac by macOS. If the language files aren't installed, macOS downloads them from Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Open in Claude and the Ask box copy a transcript and open claude.ai or the Claude app. Nothing is sent until you paste it and send it yourself.
  • Send Feedback opens a page on Audie's website in your browser. The page address includes Audie's version and your macOS version, and nothing else. GitHub hosts the page, so GitHub sees your IP address and those two version numbers when the page opens. I see nothing unless you send me an email.

Download counts, crash reports and feedback

Download counts. GitHub hosts Audie's downloads and counts how many times each file is downloaded. I look at those totals to estimate how many Macs run each version. The totals are plain numbers. They contain nothing about you or your Mac.

Crash reports, only if you turn them on. If Audie crashes, macOS gives Audie a short report after it next opens. Audie saves it on your Mac, in its own folder. Audie does not send it. If you turn on Settings › About › Crash reports, Audie asks whether you want to send the report. If you say yes, Audie copies the report and opens the feedback page. You can read it first, and you paste it and send it yourself.

A report holds the time of the crash, Audie's version, your macOS version, the type of processor in your Mac, the kind of crash, a short reason from macOS, and where in Audie's code it happened. Audie removes file and folder names from it. It never includes your recordings, transcripts or meeting names.

If you email me. I get your email address and whatever you write or attach. I use it only to reply and to fix Audie. I don't share it or add you to any list. Ask me to delete it and I will.

This website. GitHub hosts this site and sees your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. If you switch to the dark theme, your browser remembers that choice on your device.

Claude

Audie works with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. With Claude Code connected, Claude can start and stop recordings and read your transcripts through the audie command on your Mac. Whatever Claude reads is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and your Claude plan's terms, not by me. You can disconnect Claude Code any time in Settings › Claude. Audie is an independent project; it isn't made or endorsed by Anthropic.

Your control

  • Delete any meeting (Move to Trash), or delete files directly from its folder.
  • Pause a recording at any time. Nothing is saved while paused.
  • Turn live translation off in Settings › Speech.
  • Delete crash reports from Settings › About › Crash reports › Show, or turn the offer off there.
  • Uninstall: delete Audie from Applications and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Audie.

Audie for Windows (test build)

The Windows test build follows the same rules. Your audio and transcripts stay on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Audie Data. It has no account and sends nothing to me.

  • It uses the internet only to download the speech model once, from GitHub, or from Hugging Face if that fails. Whichever one serves the file sees your IP address. It does not check for updates yet.
  • It deletes recorded audio after 30 days by default, like the Mac, and keeps transcripts.
  • It keeps a log of technical events, including details of any crash. The log leaves out your user name, meeting titles and transcript text. It stays on your PC unless you email it to me.
  • To remove everything, uninstall Audie and delete the Audie Data folder.

Audie

Privacy and terms

Last updated November 14, 2026

Audie is a meeting recorder for Mac made by David Jung ("I", "me"). This page says what Audie does with your data and the terms for using it.

The short version

  • Your audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. Audie has no account, no server and no cloud storage. I never receive your recordings or transcripts.
  • Text goes to Anthropic only when Claude reads it. If you ask Claude about a meeting, that transcript text becomes part of your Claude conversation, under your agreement with Anthropic. Audie itself sends nothing to Anthropic.
  • I collect nothing about how you use Audie. No analytics, no crash reports, no tracking.

What Audie stores on your Mac

In ~/Library/Application Support/Audie/, or a meetings folder you choose in Settings › Recording:

WhatKept
Meeting audio30 days by default (7, 90 days or forever in Settings). Never deleted before its transcript is made.
Transcripts, meeting details, how you filed a meeting (section, pinned, archived)Until you delete the meeting
English translations of lines in other languages, if live translation is onUntil you delete the meeting
Settings, your custom words, an activity log (meeting titles and technical events, no transcript text)Until you delete Audie's folder
The speech model (1.5 GB)Until you delete Audie's folder

If you pick a meetings folder inside iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that service copies your recordings to its cloud under its own terms. Audie warns you when you pick one.

When Audie uses the internet

Only for these, and none of them include your recordings or transcripts:

  • Updates. About once a day Audie downloads a small update list from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • The speech model, downloaded once from GitHub, or from Hugging Face if that fails. You can also import it from a file.
  • Live translation. Done on your Mac by macOS. If the language files aren't installed, macOS downloads them from Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Open in Claude and the Ask box copy a transcript and open claude.ai or the Claude app. Nothing is sent until you paste it and send it yourself.
  • Send Feedback opens a web page with my email address. You decide what to write.

Claude

Audie works with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. With Claude Code connected, Claude can start and stop recordings and read your transcripts through the audie command on your Mac. Whatever Claude reads is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and your Claude plan's terms, not by me. You can disconnect Claude Code any time in Settings › Claude. Audie is an independent project; it isn't made or endorsed by Anthropic.

Your control

  • Delete any meeting (Move to Trash), or delete files directly from its folder.
  • Pause a recording at any time. Nothing is saved while paused.
  • Turn live translation off in Settings › Speech.
  • Uninstall: delete Audie from Applications and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Audie.
